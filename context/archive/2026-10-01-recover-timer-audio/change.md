## Notes

Reported on iPhone in Chrome: locking the phone during a running timer pauses it. Resume restores countdown without sounds. Reloading the page and starting a new workout restores sounds.

2026-10-01 device acceptance: the user tested the updated production preview on the same iPhone and confirmed that audio still works after locking, unlocking, and pressing Resume. User report: „Teraz wszystko działa okej. Po zablokowaniu ekranu i po ponownym odblokowaniu telefonu, po wciśnięciu Resume, dźwięk nadal działa”. Preview was stopped at the user's request after the test.
